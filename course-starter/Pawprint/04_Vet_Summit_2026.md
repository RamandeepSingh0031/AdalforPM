### 02 — Opening Keynote

**Name:** "The Telehealth Gap: Why Adoption Stalled — And Why the Opportunity Has Never Been Bigger"
**Meta description:** Telehealth adoption among veterinary practices dropped from 38% to 29% between 2023 and 2024. This keynote unpacks what's driving the hesitation — and what the practices gaining the most revenue right now are doing differently.
**Tags:** `keynote` `telehealth` `industry trends` `revenue growth`
**Presenter:** Dr. Sarah Chen, CEO & Co-Founder, Pawprint
**Time:** 9:00 AM – 9:45 AM
**Room:** Main Hall
**More details:** Telehealth adoption dropped from 38% in 2023 to 29.2% in 2024 — a counterintuitive trend in a market growing at nearly 20% CAGR. This keynote opens the day with a clear-eyed look at what's holding practices back and what the data says about the window of opportunity for independent practice owners who move now.
**Source:** [Owner Exchange — Veterinary Industry Trends](https://ownerexchange.com/veterinary-industry-trends/)

**Time:** 9:45 AM – 10:30 AM

Q&A in the final 10 minutes.

### 05 — Keynote: Regulatory Compliance

**Name:** "Telehealth Regulations by State: Your 2026 Compliance Playbook"
**Meta description:** A plain-English breakdown of where VCPR laws stand today, which states changed the rules in 2024–2026, and exactly what you need to do to practice telehealth legally and confidently in your state.
**Tags:** `keynote` `regulatory` `compliance` `licensing` `VCPR`
**Presenter:** Marcus Webb, Regulatory Counsel, Pawprint (Fractional)
**Time:** 11:00 AM – 11:45 AM
**Room:** Main Hall
**More details:** Twenty-two states have language explicitly requiring an in-person examination to establish a VCPR — while states including Florida, Arizona, California, and Washington DC have recently moved to allow virtual VCPR establishment under specific conditions. This session delivers a state-by-state compliance checklist attendees can take home and act on immediately.
**Sources:**
- [AVMA — VCPR Requirements](https://www.avma.org/news/vcpr-requirements-fuel-state-legislative-activity)
