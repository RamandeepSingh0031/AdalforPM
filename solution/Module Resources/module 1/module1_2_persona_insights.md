## One Shared Need

**Affordable vet care that reduces cost barriers.**

- Emma feels *guilt* over skipping vet visits due to $200-300 routine costs (`02_Personas.md` → "Persona 1: Emma" → "Pain Points")
- Marcus's primary job-to-be-done is *access affordable vet care at half the cost* (`02_Personas.md` → "Persona 2: Marcus" → "Jobs to be Done")
- This aligns with the broader market insight: **52% of pet owners skip vet care due to cost** (`01_Company_Brief.md` → "The Problem → For Pet Owners")

## One Critical Difference (Roadmap Implication)

| Persona | Usage Frequency | Trigger | Implication |
|---------|-----------------|---------|-------------|
| **Emma** | 1-2x/month | Reactive (pet shows symptom) | Needs **urgency triage + reassurance** features |
| **Marcus** | 2-4x/month | Reactive + preventive | Needs **subscription discounts + pharmacy refills** |

**Source:** `02_Personas.md` → "Pawprint Usage Pattern" for each persona

### Roadmap Decision Impact

The AI Symptom Checker (Q3 2026) directly addresses Emma's urgency uncertainty pain point — "Is this serious or can I wait?" However, Emma's reactive usage pattern (1-2x/month) means she may not convert to subscription pricing. 

Meanwhile, Marcus's higher frequency (2-4x/month) and focus on cost savings makes him a better candidate for the **repeat customer subscription option** planned for Q2 2026 (`01_Company_Brief.md` → "Product Roadmap → Q2 2026").

**Recommendation:** Position AI Symptom Checker as free funnel entry (Emma's reassurance need) → convert to subscription for repeat users (Marcus's cost-savings need).
