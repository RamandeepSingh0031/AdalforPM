## Detailed Analysis

### 1. PacVet 2026 — Sacramento, CA

**Conference Name:** Pacific Veterinary Conference  
**Organizing Body:** California Veterinary Medical Association (CVMA)  
**Website:** [pacvet.net](https://pacvet.net/)

**Relevance to Pawprint:**
- **Geographic fit:** Strong. California has the largest vet population in the US; Pawprint HQ is in SF. CVMA reaches 20,000+ veterinarians and paraprofessionals.
- **Attendee profile:** Mix of DVMs, RVTs, practice managers, and practice owners — all decision-makers for adopting telehealth.
- **New Early Career Veterinarian Track:** Perfect for Pawprint's pitch — early-career vets are more likely to adopt telehealth as a revenue stream.
- **Intimate scale:** Smaller conference means less noise, more meaningful conversations. Ideal for a first-time exhibitor.

**Sponsorship/Exhibitor Options:**
- Exhibit booth required before sponsorship
- Premier sponsor tiers: Diamond ($50K+), Sapphire ($25-49K), Emerald ($10-24K), Topaz ($6-9K)
- Benefits include logo visibility in marketing to 20K+ professionals, app push notifications, booth traffic incentives

**Estimated ROI — Vet Leads:**
- Conservative: 30-40 qualified leads (practice owners, solo practitioners)
- Optimistic: 50-60 leads with strong booth engagement + Emerald sponsor tier
- Lead quality: High (California-based, early adopter mindset)

**Why Relevant:** CVMA's marketing reach to 20,000+ CA vets aligns with Pawprint's need to expand from 120 to 320 vet partners. Early-career vet track is an ideal ICP match.

### 2. VMX 2027 — Orlando, FL

**Conference Name:** Veterinary Meeting & Expo (VMX)  
**Organizing Body:** NAVC (North American Veterinary Community)  
**Website:** [navc.com/vmx-event](https://navc.com/vmx-event/)

**Relevance to Pawprint:**
- **Scale:** World's largest veterinary conference with ~20,000 attendees and hundreds of exhibitors.
- **Timing:** January 2027 is ideal for Q1 2027 vet pipeline building.
- **Attendee mix:** Full veterinary team — DVMs, vet nurses/technicians, practice managers, hospital owners.
- **Practice Management Track:** Dedicated sessions for practice owners/managers — perfect audience for Pawprint's "expand revenue without hiring" pitch.
- **International reach:** Good for brand visibility, but may dilute US-based lead quality.

**Sponsorship/Exhibitor Options:**
- Exhibit hall access to 20,000+ prospects
- Multiple sponsorship tiers (contact NAVC for pricing)
- Exhibitor Success & ROI Center available for free training

**Estimated ROI — Vet Leads:**
- Conservative: 40-60 qualified leads (large but less targeted)
- Optimistic: 80-100 leads with premium booth placement + sponsored session
- Lead quality: Medium (national/international mix, high competition for attention)

**Why Relevant:** VMX is the premier vet event. If Pawprint wants to establish credibility and reach practice owners at scale, this is the venue. However, high competition and cost may reduce ROI efficiency.

## Recommendation

### Prioritize: **PacVet 2026 (June 18-21, Sacramento)**

**Rationale:**

1. **Highest ROI per dollar spent:** Smaller, more intimate conference with 3,000-5,000 attendees means higher-quality conversations and less competition for attention. Pawprint can stand out as a telehealth innovator rather than getting lost among 600+ exhibitors.

2. **Geographic alignment:** Pawprint is SF-based; California has the largest vet population in the US. PacVet's CVMA marketing reaches 20,000+ CA vets directly — aligning with the goal to expand from 120 to 320 vet partners.

3. **Timing fits Q2 OKR:** June 2026 falls within the Q1–Q2 Conference Initiative window. This gives Jordan time to prepare booth materials, train staff, and coordinate with Vet Relations Manager.

4. **Early Career Veterinarian Track:** New for 2026, this track targets the exact ICP who would adopt telehealth — early-career vets looking to build their practice and generate supplemental revenue.

5. **Cost-efficient test run:** PacVet's Topaz sponsorship ($6-9K) + booth is significantly cheaper than VMX or WVC sponsorship. This allows Pawprint to test conference ROI before committing to larger 2027 events.

**Estimated outcome:** 50+ vet leads at ~$200-300 per lead (assuming $15-20K total investment including booth, sponsor tier, travel, staffing). This meets the OKR target of 50+ high-quality leads in a single event.

### Secondary: **WVC Nashville 2026 (August 15-18)**

If PacVet performs well, WVC Nashville in August 2026 offers a follow-up opportunity to expand into underserved Midwest/Southeast markets.

### Long-term: **VMX 2027 (January 16-20, Orlando)**

Reserve VMX 2027 for major brand visibility if Pawprint's vet network reaches 250+ partners by Q4 2026. The scale and cost require a more mature go-to-market presence.
