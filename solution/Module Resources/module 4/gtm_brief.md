# GTM Content Brief: PawPrint Vet Summit 2026 Pre-Event Push
**Date:** March 18, 2026
**POC:** Marketing / Strategy
**Status:** Draft for Sprint 1

## TL;DR
A high-signal, peer-to-peer marketing campaign targeting independent and multi-location clinic owners to drive 75+ qualified registrations for the San Francisco Summit by highlighting revenue opportunities and peer success.

---

## 1. Campaign Goals & Success Metrics
*   **Primary Goal:** Drive high-intent registrations for the PawPrint Vet Summit 2026.
*   **Success Metric:** 75+ Qualified Registrations (defined as practice owners/decision-makers completing the full registration form).
*   **Secondary Metric:** 20+ Applications for the "Invite-Only Office Hours" session (indicates high-intent lead quality).
*   *Note: We are optimizing for attendee quality and registration conversion, not top-of-funnel impressions.*

## 2. Target Audience
*   **Independent Practice Owners:** Solo practitioners looking for work-life balance and ways to compete with corporate groups.
*   **Multi-location Clinic Owners:** Owners (like Dr. Amanda Foster) focused on scale, standardized compliance, and incremental revenue across multiple sites.

## 3. Brand Voice & Positioning
*   **Tone:** Credible, direct, peer-to-peer.
*   **Guideline:** Speak "Vet-to-Vet." Avoid marketing fluff or "disruptive" tech jargon. Focus on clinical accuracy, operational reality, and hard numbers. Vets trust colleagues who have "scrubs on the ground."

## 4. Messaging Hierarchy
1.  **The Revenue Gap is Real:** Telehealth adoption dropped from 38% to 29% (2023-24), but the market is growing at 20% CAGR. Moving now is a competitive advantage for independent practices.
2.  **Evidence-Based Success:** Don't take our word for it—hear from Dr. Amanda Foster, who added $10k/month in revenue while cutting on-call hours by 40%.
3.  **One Day for Your Future:** A single day in San Francisco (Q3 2026) provides a state-by-state compliance playbook and a roadmap to the 2030 veterinary landscape.

*   **Adoption Trend:** Telehealth adoption fell from 38% (2023) to 29.2% (2024). [Source: Owner Exchange]
