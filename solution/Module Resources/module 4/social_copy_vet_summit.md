## Strategic Hooks & Reasoning

1. **Dr. Sarah Chen**
   * **Hook:** Telehealth adoption dropped from 38% to 29.2% (2023–2024), despite the market growing at 20% CAGR.
   * **Reasoning:** It's a counterintuitive, pattern-breaking stat. Highlighting a drop in adoption during a market boom creates an information gap that makes practice owners want to know what they are missing.

2. **Dr. Amanda Foster**
   * **Hook:** Burnout costs the veterinary industry $1–$2 billion annually.
   * **Reasoning:** Burnout is the most visceral, emotional pain point for independent clinic owners. Pairing this massive industry cost with her specific, achievable outcome (cutting on-call hours by 40% while adding $10k/month) moves the conversation from abstract problem to tangible solution.

3. **Marcus Webb**
   * **Hook:** 22 states require an in-person VCPR, but major states are rapidly rewriting the rules.
   * **Reasoning:** Regulatory confusion is the primary barrier to entry for telehealth. A "Playbook" that simplifies state-by-state compliance is highly actionable and promises immediate value for attending.

4. **Dr. Priya Nair**
   * **Hook:** Projected deficit of 70,092 veterinarians by 2032.
   * **Reasoning:** A terrifying macro trend for hiring. Flipping this into a competitive advantage for tech-enabled, independent practices provides a compelling, optimistic reason to attend her session.
